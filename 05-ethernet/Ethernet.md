# Ethernet

Ethernet is a family of wired local-network standards. It defines frames, MAC addressing, cabling, and signaling.

- Standard Ethernet: 10 Mbps
- Fast Ethernet (`100BASE-TX`): 100 Mbps
- Gigabit Ethernet (`1000BASE-T`): 1 Gbps
- 10 Gigabit Ethernet: 10 Gbps

Switches use MAC addresses to forward Ethernet frames inside a local network. The actual speed depends on hardware, cable, and negotiated link capability.
